# MoonXprojecT Enterprise V24

## Attendance & Shift Engine

V24 adds a production foundation for:

- Shift master and overnight/overnight-safe scheduling
- Employee/date shift assignments
- Tolerance / grace-period metadata
- Holiday calendar
- Attendance calculation snapshots
- Late, early-leave, worked-minute and overtime-minute calculation
- Effective workday calculation
- Attendance adjustment workflow
- Permissions for shift, holiday, calculation and adjustment approval

Migration: `027_v24_attendance_shift_engine.sql`

Important: attendance and overtime rules are configurable and should be aligned with company policy and applicable Indonesian employment regulations before production.
